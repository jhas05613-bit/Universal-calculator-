1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Universal Calculator World ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and use Universal Calculator World on Android devices.

We respect your personal privacy. We do not require you to create an account, register an email address, or provide personal identity details to use the calculator features.

Summary: The app provides three core offline calculators: Basic Calculator, Percentage Calculator, and Age & Date Calculator. Other specialized tools may require an internet connection. Online tools may display Google AdMob advertisements.

2.1 Offline Core

The Basic Calculator, Percentage Calculator, and Age & Date Calculator are designed to perform their core calculations locally without requiring an internet connection.

2.2 Online Tools

Other specialized tools may require an active internet connection to obtain external or current data and to provide online services.

3. Information Collection & Processing

The application does not require users to create an account or provide personal identity information to use its calculator features.

When online services or advertisements are used, third-party services may automatically process technical information necessary to provide those services.

3.1 Automatically Processed Technical Information

  • IP address
  • Device and operating-system information
  • Application version
  • Screen and language information
  • Advertising identifiers where applicable
  • Network and request information

Such information may be processed by third-party service providers according to their respective privacy policies.

4. Local Data, History & Preferences

Certain application settings and calculator information may be stored locally on the device to provide the application's features.

  • Calculator history, where available
  • Theme preference
  • Haptic feedback preference
  • Button sound preference
  • Default calculator preference
  • Default currency preference
  • Number formatting preferences
  • Other application settings

Local application data is stored on the user's device and is not intentionally uploaded to a remote server by the calculator functionality.

5. Google AdMob & Advertising

Universal Calculator World may use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

5.1 Advertisement Types

  • Banner Ads: May appear on eligible online-only calculator screens.
  • Interstitial Ads: May be displayed after a completed use of an eligible online-only calculator.
  • Offline Calculator Exclusion: Basic Calculator, Percentage Calculator, and Age & Date Calculator are intended to remain ad-free.

5.2 Test Ads and Production Ads

During development and testing, official Google AdMob test advertisements may be used. Before production release, test advertisement units may be replaced with the application's production AdMob advertisement units.

5.3 Advertising Information

Google and its advertising partners may process technical information, advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and advertising interaction information for purposes such as advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service improvement, subject to applicable Google policies.

6. Third-Party APIs

Some online tools may connect to third-party services to obtain external or current information required for calculations.

For example, the Currency Converter may use a third-party exchange-rate service to obtain currency exchange-rate information.

The application is designed not to intentionally send personal identity information as part of a standard currency conversion request.

Third-party services may process technical network information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

The application may use permissions required for its online and interactive features.

  • Internet: Required for online calculators, external data services, and advertisements.
  • Network State: May be used to determine whether online functionality is available.
  • Haptic Feedback: The app may use the device vibration/haptic mechanism to provide tactile feedback when buttons and controls are used.

Haptic feedback does not record or transmit haptic information. Users can disable haptic feedback through the application's settings when the option is available.

8. Children's Privacy

Universal Calculator World is a general-purpose utility application and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

We do not intentionally request personal identity information from children through the application.

9. Data Security & Storage

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application and use secure network connections where supported by third-party services.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention & User Controls

Locally stored application data may remain on the device until the user clears application data or uninstalls the application.

Users can manage available application settings and locally stored information using the controls provided by the application and Android operating system.

11. Third-Party Privacy Policies

The application may use third-party services that operate under their own privacy policies.
